SAK — Solana Agent Kernel

Logo Mark Exploration

Three geometric concepts for the SAK icon mark. All flat, 2-color max, tested at 16px favicon scale and 512px hero scale.

No gradients No letterforms No shadows Max 2 colors Favicon-safe #00e87a / #ffffff on #080810
Concept 01
Kill Gate
Favicon
32px
16px
Octagonal frame references authority and finality — the geometry of a stop sign, recast as a security boundary. The horizontal dashes flanking a center node read as a circuit breaker: something in a live circuit that can be interrupted at will. At 16px the octagon collapses cleanly to a bold geometric square with a bright center dot.
Concept 02
Circuit Hex
Favicon
32px
16px
Hexagon outer ring references the Solana ecosystem — on-chain, cryptographic, six-sided like a validator cluster. The inner diamond/kernel is the solid core: the SAK runtime sitting inside the chain, waiting to intercept. Two distinct shapes with high contrast. At 16px the diamond stays legible as a bright fill inside a dark outline.
Concept 03
Signal Cut
Favicon
32px
16px
Two parallel rails represent the Solana transaction pipeline — slots moving through time. The angled intercept bar physically cuts both rails before they complete, halting the signal. The 7° tilt breaks symmetry, suggesting active intervention rather than passive blockage. The most literal visualization of "pre-sign kill switch" in a single mark. Boldest at 16px.
